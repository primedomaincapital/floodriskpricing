Flood risk resources

A starting set of independent tools and reading for anyone trying to understand flood risk and flood insurance pricing in the United States. These are outside sites, listed for reference only; listing here does not imply any connection with this site or with Prime Domain Capital.

Websites and tools

  • FEMA Flood Map Service Center — The official federal map service for finding a property's flood map and flood hazard products.
  • FloodSmart — The official National Flood Insurance Program site for quotes and online policy purchase.
  • First Street — Property-level flood risk scores and reports based on a model that includes rainfall, rivers, tides, and storm surge.
  • First Street Flood Factor FAQ — Answers on how Flood Factor scores are calculated and what they do and do not represent.
  • FloodPrice, Nebraska flood insurance — Nebraska flood insurance pricing data, with statewide, county, and city averages.

Industry groups and associations

  • Association of State Floodplain Managers — The association of state floodplain managers, with background on floodplain management practice.

Articles and reading

  • FEMA launches an online option to buy a flood insurance policy — The October 2026 announcement of a new online option for buying a National Flood Insurance Program policy.
  • How to read flood risk data on property listings — A guide to reading FEMA flood zones and First Street scores on property listings.
  • Flood risk resources, a plain-language guide — A plain-language guide to flood risk resources and insurance options from a flood insurance brokerage's blog.
  • Flood Insurance 2026: NFIP vs Private — WalletGrower — National averages for NFIP ($926/yr) and private cover ($1,140/yr), Risk Rating 2.0 effects, and when each wins.
  • Flood Insurance in 2026: Zones, Costs, Coverage & Lender Rules — First Commerce Financial — Zone-by-zone costs, the 18% annual cap, and private flood's rise to about 27% of premiums.
  • How much does flood insurance cost? — FloodZone Pro — 2026 cost ranges by FEMA zone for NFIP and private cover, plus Community Rating System discounts.
